Open these sections, not front pages

Front pages are event reporting. These sections are argument pieces — one mechanism each. Tap, scan three headlines, apply the filter, pick one.

This week, look for

I can't see inside the Economist, WSJ, Mint or ET — they block automated access entirely. So instead of naming articles, here is what to scan for when you open those sections this week. If a headline touches one of these, it is worth your gap.

1. The final NBFC revolving-credit rule. RBI's draft proposal to ban flexi-credit already cost Bajaj Finance ₹33,500 cr in a day, and comments stay open until 28 August. Watch for signs of the final rule's shape, and whether adjacent products like supply-chain financing get swept in.

2. Q1FY27 results season, watching for the operating-leverage pattern. Hindalco's EBITDA margin expanded 419 bps on 32% revenue growth; SBI's operating profit grew slower than NII. Track whether more Q1 results show costs growing faster or slower than revenue before drawing a sector-wide conclusion.

3. The IDBI Bank stake sale. Government sources expect it to close this fiscal year, adding $2.5 bn to a disinvestment target it's already on track to beat. Watch for a formal announcement or buyer name.

4. Whether Sarvam AI's markup is company-specific or sector-wide. Its valuation moved 5.3% in about a month on Nvidia's participation alone. Watch whether Krutrim or other Indian AI startups get similar markups soon, which would tell you investors are repricing the whole sector, not just betting on one company.

Capture — one line, right now
